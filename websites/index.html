Questions, answered

Clear answers before you commit.

Do I need a multi-page website?

Where different services or search intents deserve their own useful pages, yes. A small site is better than many thin pages.

Will you guarantee Google rankings?

No. Codeedge builds strong technical and content foundations but does not guarantee rankings.

Can the site connect to CRM and WhatsApp?

Yes, where the agreed setup supports it.

A website built around the enquiry journey

A useful service website explains what you do, who it is for, what happens next and how to contact the business. Build important information into accessible HTML, keep navigation simple and make phone and WhatsApp routes easy to use on mobile.

For UK and Pakistan projects, agree language, currency, service coverage and contact hours before writing pages. Use actual business facts and approved pricing. Connect forms and contact routes to your lead-management process so an enquiry has an owner after it arrives.
