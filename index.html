What is Codeedge?

Codeedge is a connected growth and operating system for service businesses, bringing visibility, enquiries, lead handling, communication, bookings and follow-up into one clearer journey.

Is Codeedge just a website or SEO agency?

No. Websites and local SEO are part of the system, but Codeedge is designed around the full customer journey.

Does AI replace staff?

No. AI is used for repetitive first-response and workflow support while important decisions remain visible to the owner and team.

Do you guarantee rankings or revenue?

No. Codeedge uses evidence-led language and does not guarantee Google rankings, revenue or lead volume.
