The problem

Running the business is hard enough.

When you're up a ladder, under a sink, or on-site with a customer, you can't always get to the phone. A weak website, slow replies and poor Google visibility don't just look untidy — they can quietly cost you jobs to a competitor who answered first.

Invisible on Google

If your business doesn't show up for local searches, customers simply find someone who does.

Outdated or missing website

An old, slow, or non-existent website makes it harder for people to trust you before they've even called.

Missed enquiries

Every call or message you can't answer while on-site is a potential job that may go elsewhere.

No organised follow-up

Without a simple system, new enquiries get scribbled down, forgotten, or lost between jobs.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to be technical to work with Codeedge?

No. We explain everything in plain English and handle the technical setup for you — you focus on running your business, we focus on the digital side.

How long does it take to get my website live?

It depends on the scope, but most websites are built and ready to launch within a few weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline before any work begins.

What is local SEO and why does it matter?

Local SEO is the work that helps your business appear when nearby customers search for services like yours on Google. Better local visibility means more of the right people finding you first.

How does the AI website assistant work?

It sits on your website and can answer common questions, capture a customer's details, and help qualify what they need — then hands the conversation over to you or your team when it matters most.

Can WhatsApp automation use our existing business number?

In most cases, yes. We'll talk through your current setup and confirm exactly how it will work for your business before anything changes.

Is there a long contract?

No. Our plans are monthly with no long lock-in contracts, so you can scale up, down, or pause as your business needs change.
