The problem

Good work does not automatically become visible proof.

Customers may be happy, but if review requests happen randomly—or not at all—the online reputation can lag behind the real service quality.

  • Review requests depend on memory
  • Positive customer experiences are not followed up
  • Different staff ask in different ways
  • The business has no clear view of reputation activity
The Codeedge approach

Create a simple, honest review workflow.

Codeedge can connect completed work to a consistent review request process while keeping customer choice and platform rules intact.

The system supports your team. It does not hide important decisions from them.
