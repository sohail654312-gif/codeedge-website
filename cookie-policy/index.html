Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Current cookie position

The website works without optional analytics. Google Analytics 4 loads only after you choose Accept analytics. Reject analytics leaves it off. Advertising storage and personalisation remain denied.

Remembering your choice

We store your analytics choice in this browser’s local storage for up to 180 days so you do not need to answer on every page. This preference is used to respect your selection.

Analytics cookies

After acceptance, Google Analytics may set _ga and _ga_ cookies to distinguish visits and maintain session information. We configure them for up to 180 days. Cookie settings in the footer lets you accept or reject analytics at any time. Rejection after acceptance disables collection, clears accessible Analytics cookies and reloads the page.

External services

When you choose to open an external service such as WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok, that service may use cookies under its own policy.

What is measured

Optional analytics measures page visits and contact-button actions. It does not receive qualification-form answers or WhatsApp message text from our custom events. See our privacy policy for details.
