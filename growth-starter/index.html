Codeedge Growth Starter

A preview portal for clinics and small local businesses that want a clearer view of their growth work and enquiries.

What Growth Starter is designed to organise

Growth Starter brings business profile information, enquiries, enquiry status, content requests and a website and local SEO roadmap into a mobile-first workspace. The current preview includes a sample clinic workspace with illustrative data.

It is an early preview, rather than a production-accepted service. Discuss suitability and the next steps with Codeedge before relying on it for live customer operations.

Keep expectations clear

The current preview does not include live Google performance metrics, automatic AI publishing, direct WhatsApp workflows, payments or production integration with Business OS and CIGO. Sample data is not evidence of customer results.

Growth Starter is not a patient-record system. Do not submit patient records or sensitive clinical information. Production use requires a separate scope and acceptance review.

Choose a starting point around your business

Tell us which services you provide, how enquiries arrive and which growth tasks are hard to track. We can assess whether the preview fits your needs or whether a focused website, local SEO or CRM setup is more appropriate.

Ask about Growth Starter through the Codeedge contact route. Scope, availability and pricing are confirmed separately; no new price is implied by this preview page.
