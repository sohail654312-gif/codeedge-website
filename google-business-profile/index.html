The problem

A profile can be visible and still underperform.

Incomplete information, weak service relevance, inconsistent updates and disconnected review activity can make the profile harder to trust and harder to act on.

  • Business information does not match the website
  • Services are vague or incomplete
  • Reviews are not supported by a consistent process
  • Customers find the profile but the next action is unclear
