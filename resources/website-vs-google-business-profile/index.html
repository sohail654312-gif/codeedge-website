Website vs Google Business Profile: what does a local service business need?

For most established local service businesses, the useful question is not website or Google Business Profile. The two play different roles and can reinforce each other.

What Google Business Profile is good at

Google Business Profile is a major local discovery and trust surface. It can show core business details, reviews, photos, service information and routes to call or visit the website.

Its strength is proximity to local search behaviour. Its limitation is control: the business operates inside Google's platform, interface and rules.

  • Local discovery
  • Reviews and visual proof
  • Quick contact actions
  • Basic business and service information

What the website is good at

A website gives the business more control over how services, areas, proof and conversion paths are explained. It can answer detailed search intent and support organic visibility beyond the profile itself.

It is also the place where the business can design a clearer commercial journey: different service pages, enquiry forms, booking pathways, FAQs and lead capture.

  • Owned service explanations
  • Broader search architecture
  • Controlled conversion paths
  • Deeper trust and qualification

Why connected usually beats isolated

When the website and Google Business Profile use accurate, consistent business information and explain the same real services, customers experience a more coherent journey. The profile can create discovery; the website can provide depth; calls or forms can create an enquiry; the CRM can support follow-up.

This is the Codeedge principle: the channels matter, but the connection between them matters too.

  • Keep business information consistent
  • Link to the most relevant pages where appropriate
  • Make contact actions obvious
  • Track what happens after the click or call

Practical next step

Check whether your website and Google profile tell the same story about services, locations and contact routes. Fix inconsistencies before adding more channels.

FAQ
Which is more important?

It depends on how customers currently discover and evaluate the business. They serve different functions.

Can I rank locally without a website?

Some businesses can gain local visibility without a strong website, but a website can add relevance, depth and conversion control.

Should the website and profile use identical wording everywhere?

They should be consistent in facts and positioning, but each surface should be written for its own user context rather than mechanically duplicated.
