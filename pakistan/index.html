Websites and business automation for Pakistan

Connect a clear business website with WhatsApp enquiries, organised lead records and practical follow-up. Codeedge scopes the setup around the way your team works.

Start with the customer journey you already have

If customers discover you through referrals or social media and then enquire through WhatsApp, a useful first step is to connect those enquiries with a clear service page and a lead record. Record who will reply, what information is needed and the next action.

For appointment-led businesses, begin with a booking request and staff confirmation. For project-based services, capture the type of work, location and callback details before preparing a quote.

Agree local requirements before implementation

  • Confirm whether your customers need English, Urdu or another language, and which language support can be delivered.
  • Set contact hours and follow-up times in Pakistan Standard Time where appropriate.
  • Agree currency, billing arrangements and provider usage charges in the proposal.
  • Check the availability of your phone, booking, CRM and messaging integrations.

UK package prices shown elsewhere on this website do not define a Pakistan quotation. Ask for a scope that matches your business and existing tools.

Build visibility from real services

Write service descriptions around what you deliver and the areas you actually serve. Publish accurate contact details and a clear enquiry route. A local business listing requires real eligibility; an online-only operation should not invent an address.
