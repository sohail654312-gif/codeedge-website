Questions from clinics
Will AI replace the receptionist?

No. The intended role is to support repetitive administrative work and busy periods while staff handle relationships, judgement and sensitive issues.

Can the AI give medical advice?

It should not be configured to replace qualified clinical judgement or provide unsafe medical guidance.

Can it handle appointment changes?

Appropriate booking and rescheduling workflows can be supported where the clinic's rules and systems allow it.
