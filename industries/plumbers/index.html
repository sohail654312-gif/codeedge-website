Questions from plumbers
Can Codeedge answer emergency calls?

An AI receptionist can support first-response workflows where configured, but genuine emergencies and safety-critical matters should follow appropriate escalation.

Will this replace the plumber's phone?

No. The aim is to reduce missed opportunity and improve visibility, not remove direct customer contact.

Can it help with quote follow-up?

Yes. Quote requests and next actions can be kept visible instead of relying on memory.
