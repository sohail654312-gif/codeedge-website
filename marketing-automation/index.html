AI marketing automation for small businesses

Connect marketing activity with the enquiries your team can actually handle. Start with a useful follow-up process, then add automation where it saves repetition.

Make follow-up useful to the customer

A marketing workflow starts with a reason to contact someone. A requested quote, an unanswered enquiry or an agreed reminder needs a different response from a general campaign. Decide the audience, trigger and next action before choosing software.

For example, a quote request can create a CRM task for the owner, prepare a reply for review and record the next agreed contact date. The purpose is a relevant conversation, rather than sending more messages.

Where AI can support the workflow

  • Prepare draft replies using approved service information.
  • Help organise content ideas around real customer questions.
  • Summarise an enquiry so the team can choose the next action.
  • Suggest follow-up tasks for a person to review.

Publishing, messaging channels and integrations depend on the agreed implementation. Human review remains important for accuracy, tone, audience selection and exceptions.

Agree permissions, limits and measurement

Confirm who may be contacted, their communication preferences, the appropriate channel and how to stop messages. Do not treat possession of a phone number as permission for a campaign. Keep customer details out of public content and use only approved business facts.

Measure response time, completed follow-ups and qualified enquiries against a recorded starting point. Campaign results depend on the offer, audience and execution; Codeedge does not promise a volume of leads or revenue.
