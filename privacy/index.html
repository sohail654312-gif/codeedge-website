Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Information you provide

This may include your name, business name, business email, phone number, business type and information about your current enquiry process.

Qualification form

The current qualification flow prepares the information you enter into a WhatsApp message. You can review that message before sending it. The website does not claim a request has been received until you choose to send it.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and access requests.
  • To understand business needs and recommend an appropriate scope.
  • To provide and support agreed Codeedge services.

Third-party services

If you use WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok links, those platforms process data under their own policies.

Optional website analytics

If you accept analytics, this website loads Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and use of phone, WhatsApp and request-preparation controls. Google processes technical information such as browser and device information and online identifiers to provide these reports. Analytics is off until you accept, and advertising consent remains denied.

Our custom analytics events do not include your name, email, phone number, form answers or WhatsApp message text. We remove query strings and fragments from the page and referrer URLs supplied by our analytics code. Preparing a WhatsApp request is measured separately from a confirmed enquiry; it does not prove that a message was sent.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. Analytics cookies are configured for up to 180 days. Choose Cookie settings in the footer to change your preference. Rejecting after accepting disables collection, clears the Google Analytics cookies accessible to this website and reloads the page. Google describes its own processing in its privacy policy.
